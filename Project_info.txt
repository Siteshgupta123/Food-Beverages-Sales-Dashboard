🚀 Project:
Food and Beverage Sales Analysis Dashboard
========================================
📌 Tool Used:
- Microsoft Power BI
- Excel
========================================
📊 Objective:
- To analyze and visualize sales data across different time periods, product categories, and sales channels.
- To identify trends in order volumes and revenue.
- To evaluate sales performance of products and salespersons for strategic decision-making.
========================================
📈 Key Metrics:
- Total Products: 798
- Total Orders: 53K
- Total Revenue: $17.9M
========================================
📅 Time Analysis:
- Monthly Revenue & Orders: Identifies high-performing months (e.g., October & November).
- Quarterly Analysis: Q3 & Q4 show the highest sales peaks.
========================================
📦 Product Insights:
- Top Product Group: Wheat Flour
- Other High Performers: Oil, Yeasts, Flour, Liquor
- Product Category Split:
  - Drink: $16.37M (91.39%)
  - Food: $1.54M (8.61%)
========================================
📦 Channel Performance:
- Online: $8.70M (48.56%)
- Distributor: $6.10M (34.05%)
- Retail: $3.11M (17.39%)
========================================
👨‍💼 Salesperson Performance:
- Carla F.: Highest revenue generator
- Julio Li. & Gustavo: Top in order volume
========================================
🧠 Business Use Cases:
- Optimize stock and supply chain based on top-selling products.
- Adjust marketing strategies depending on high/low-performing channels.
- Incentivize top-performing salespeople.
